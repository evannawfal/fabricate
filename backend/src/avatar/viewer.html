Move mode (M): drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom; clicks never place points.
Pick mode (P): orbit/pan are locked, scroll still zooms; a single click on the garment sets the highlighted point. Click a point's name to re-pick just that one.
Edit a point's x / y / z (type, or arrow keys) and its marker moves live. Left/Right are the wearer's own sides (the wearer's left appears on screen-right when viewed from the front). Sleeve openings are optional, but set both or neither.
